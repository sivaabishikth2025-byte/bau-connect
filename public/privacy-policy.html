Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

1. Who we are

BAU Connect (“we,” “us,” or “the app”) is a campus connection platform for Bay Atlantic University students and staff. The service is available at baustudentconnect.com and through related mobile apps that load the same service.

Contact: techconnect@bau.edu

2. Who can use BAU Connect

Access is limited to people with a verified @stu.bau.edu or @bau.edu email address. The app is intended for the BAU campus community, not for children under 13.

3. Information we collect

  • Account information: email, display name, age, gender, major, university, bio, interests, and preferences you provide.
  • Photos: profile and gallery images you upload.
  • Activity content: feed posts, comments, likes, joins, volunteer interest, and related timestamps.
  • Messages: direct messages you send to connections.
  • Notifications: in-app inbox items and optional push/email notifications.
  • Technical data: basic device/browser information needed to run the service securely.
  • Map usage: third-party map providers may process location-related requests you initiate. We do not sell your precise location.

4. How we use information

  • Create and manage your account
  • Show profiles, feed posts, maps, and connections
  • Deliver messages and notifications
  • Moderate content and keep the campus community safe
  • Operate, secure, debug, and improve the service
  • Comply with law and enforce our terms

We do not sell your personal information.

5. How information is shared

We share information only as needed to run the service with other signed-in campus users as designed by the product, and with processors such as Firebase, Cloudinary, email providers, Netlify hosting, and map providers. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect users, BAU, or the service.

6. Data safety & security

We use industry-standard providers and transmit data over HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

7. Retention

We keep your account and content while your account is active. If you delete your account or request deletion, we remove or anonymize personal data within a reasonable period, except where limited records must be retained for security, abuse prevention, or legal reasons.

8. Your choices & rights

  • Edit profile information and photos in the app
  • Control notification permissions on your device
  • Request account deletion at baustudentconnect.com/delete-account or by emailing techconnect@bau.edu from your BAU email

9. Children’s privacy

BAU Connect is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Cookies & similar tech

The website and app may use essential cookies or local storage for login sessions and basic functionality. We do not use third-party advertising cookies to track you across other sites for ads.

11. International users

The service is operated for Bay Atlantic University in Washington, D.C., United States. If you access it from elsewhere, your information may be processed in the United States.

12. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.

13. Contact

Questions about privacy: techconnect@bau.edu

Full interactive policy: baustudentconnect.com/privacy
